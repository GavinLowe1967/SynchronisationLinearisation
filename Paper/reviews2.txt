> ## 2. Inaccurate Comparison with CA Linearizability (p. 14, ll. 14-36)
> 
> The claim that the authors' definition is "equivalent to [CA
> linearizability] when $S$ is taken to be the inverse image under the

That's not quite right, because the synchronisation specification object can
define the return values.  However, the two definitions aren't quite
equivalent because CA linearisation allows specifications that, e.g., allow
threads A and B to synchronise, but not threads A and C (essentially because
the specification is in terms of an *arbitrary* set of CA-traces).  

We have given a more detailed comparison, in particular showing that they are
equivalent when the set-linearisation specification doesn't impose
thread-specific conditions like in the previous paragraph.

that we consider our approach to be more clear and straightforward.  

> Furthermore, subsequent work on
